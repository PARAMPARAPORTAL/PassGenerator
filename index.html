What is Parampara?

Parampara is the grand installation ceremony of the Board of Directors of the Lions Club of Howrah for the Lionistic Year 2026–27.

The name 'Parampara' was thoughtfully chosen because its very meaning reflects the essence of this installation tradition beautifully—an unbroken lineage, a seamless transfer of responsibility, and a legacy passed forward from one generation to the next.

By Women

This year marks a powerful chapter where leadership is entrusted to women carrying forward a legacy built over generations. A testament to progress, strength, and unwavering dedication to service, celebrating the powerful spirit of female leadership.
